Providers

Tokenxiety calls each provider with the cookies in your existing browser session. No tokens are stored.

Privacy

  • Host access is limited to claude.ai, chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com.
  • Only sanitized utilization numbers are stored locally; raw responses, prompts, and tokens are not.
  • Cookies are sent by the browser; the extension never reads them directly.
